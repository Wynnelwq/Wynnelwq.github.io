I am a master's student in **Computational Communication** at the School of Journalism and Communication, [Nanjing University](https://www.nju.edu.cn/). I came to communication research from computer science — I studied Computer Science and Technology as an undergraduate — so I build my own data collection pipelines before asking theoretically motivated questions of the data they produce.

> My research asks how algorithmic gatekeeping — increasingly generative-AI gatekeeping — decides which information sources become visible, and what that means for public communication.

My current project studies AI-generated search summaries on Weibo. I collect the full text of the AI answers together with every source they cite, and analyse the resulting source structure in order to characterise how generative search selects, aggregates, and ranks the voices it amplifies.

## Research Interests

- **Computational Communication** — using large-scale digital trace data and computational methods to study communication processes and effects.
- **Social Media** — information production, diffusion, and the distribution of attention on platforms such as Weibo.
- **Causal Inference** — difference-in-differences (DID) and propensity score matching DID (PSM-DID) designs for identifying policy- and platform-level effects in observational data.
- **Web Crawling** — building reproducible scrapers and parsing pipelines that turn platform interfaces into analysable datasets.
- **Algorithmic Gatekeeping (AI Gatekeeping)** — how recommendation and generative search systems determine which sources, stakeholders, and viewpoints reach an audience.
